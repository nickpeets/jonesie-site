About Us

the story behind the shop
A little about meI'm the heart of jonesie — here's the story of how the shop came to be.

jonesie started the way the best small things do — quietly, and out of love for the hunt.

What began as weekends spent digging through estate sales and forgotten corners turned into a small, deliberate shop built on a simple idea: that a good object deserves a second life and an honest description. Every piece here was found, cleaned, photographed, and written up by hand.

We're not interested in moving fast or selling everything. We're interested in the right piece finding the right person — and in being straight with you about condition, measurements, and what you're actually getting. Eight photos. Real notes. No surprises.

Thank you for being here. Wander a while — jonesie's glad to have you.
